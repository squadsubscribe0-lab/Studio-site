About

Hi, I’m an Indie Developer

I make games on my own — from the first rough idea to the build that ends up in players’ hands. Every step is mine: designing the core loop, building it in Unity, writing the code, crafting the UI, optimising performance and preparing the game for release.

I focus on casual, hypercasual and idle games because they reward clarity. A good one is understood in seconds, feels great to play right away and always gives you a reason to come back. That’s the bar I aim for with every project.

Launch isn’t the finish line. I watch how people actually play, listen to feedback and keep iterating — small, deliberate changes that make each game better over time.

  • Game designCore loops, progression and balancing
  • Unity developmentGameplay systems, scenes and builds
  • ProgrammingClean, maintainable C#
  • UIClear menus and satisfying feedback
  • OptimizationSmooth on mobile and the web
  • PublishingStore and web-portal releases
  • Player feedbackIterating on what players actually do

How I work

My Philosophy

01

Keep it simple

“Easy to understand. Hard to master.”

If a player needs a tutorial to start having fun, the design isn’t done yet.

02

Focus on fun

“If it’s not fun, it doesn’t work.”

Every feature has to earn its place by making the moment-to-moment play feel better.

03

Iterate & improve

“Small changes, big results.”

Playtest, measure, adjust, repeat. Steady polish beats big rewrites.
