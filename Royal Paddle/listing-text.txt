GAME NAME
Paddle Royale

CATEGORY / TAGS
Category: Casual (or Arcade)
Tags: pong, multiplayer, battle royale, 2 player, paddle, ball, arcade, io, friends, team

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Pong meets battle royale! 8 paddles fight in one arena. Lose all your hearts and you're out. The last paddle standing wins.

DESCRIPTION
Paddle Royale is a fast pong battle royale where 8 paddles share one arena. Guard your goal and send the ball into your rivals' goals. Every goal you let in costs you a heart. When a player is knocked out, the arena shrinks. Knocked-out players can still drop Wells and Bars on the players who are left.

Create a room and invite your friends with a link or a 5-letter code. Play free-for-all with up to 8 players or teams from 1v1 to 4v4, and bots fill any empty seats. You can also jump into a quick match against bots, or set up your own practice match. Grab power-ups like Grow, Multi-ball, Shield, Slow-mo and Reverse, and fight in 6 arenas with pillars, spinners, bumpers and gravity wells. Earn coins in every match and spend them on paddles, balls, trails and avatars in the shop.

CONTROLS
Mouse: move your paddle
A / D or Left / Right arrow keys: move your paddle
Touch: drag anywhere on the screen
Esc / P: pause

FEATURES
- 8-paddle pong battle royale
- Play with friends: private rooms, invite links and room codes
- Quick preset chat (no free text)
- Custom practice matches, including team games from 1v1 to 4v4
- 8 power-ups and 6 arenas
- Shop with paddle, ball, trail and avatar skins
- Works on desktop and mobile, in portrait and landscape
