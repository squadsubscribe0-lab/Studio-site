# Piñata Payday: resubmission (Guest Wishes version)

## What changed since the rejected build
- **New core loop: Guest Wishes.** Three kids stand at the bottom of the screen, and each one wishes for a specific piñata, like "Red", "Blue", "⭐ Star", "🐴 Big Donkey" or "💣 Bomb". Every wish has a timer.
- Piñatas now come in 4 colors (red, blue, green, yellow), so the player has to **pick the right target** instead of hitting anything.
- A granted wish gives a candy burst, **+2 arm stamina** and a point toward the party goal. Smashing a piñata that matches two wishes grants both, and beating a boss piñata grants every open wish.
- **Progression:** 7 parties (Mia's Birthday → Mayor's Mega Fiesta). Grant enough wishes in one day to finish a party and get 1–3 stars. Finishing a party unlocks new piñatas, skills and places.
- The bill, rent, debt, due date and late tax systems were **removed**. Cash is only spent on upgrades now.
- New achievement: "Wish Maker" (grant 50 wishes). Saves from the old build still load.

## Form fields (copy-paste)
**Title:** Piñata Payday

**Short description:**
Every kid at the party has a wish. Smash the right piñata, grant wishes and finish every party with three stars.

**Description:**
The party is on, and every kid wants something different. One wants a red piñata, another wants a star and another wants the big donkey. Swing at piñatas hanging from their ropes and smash the ones the kids wish for before they run out of patience. Every granted wish sends out a burst of candy and cash, and the happy kid hands you a juice box that gives your tired arm some stamina back.

Grant enough wishes to finish each party, from Mia's little birthday to the Mayor's Mega Fiesta, and aim for three stars. Start with little piñatas that pop in a few hits, then take on big donkeys, golden jackpots, bombs that chain-react and armored piñatas. Hit fast for double and triple cash combos, and beat a giant boss piñata every third day to grant every wish at once.

Grow a skill tree with 16 skills, buy and upgrade 7 weapons (from a frying pan to a sledgehammer and a golden bat), grab boosts for tomorrow, and find 16 collectibles in gift boxes to complete sets with permanent bonuses. Unlock a birthday bash, a beach party and a spooky night, earn 14 achievements, and come back daily for a bigger bonus and your Piñata Factory earnings.

**Controls:**
- Mouse: click or hold on a piñata to swing the bat
- Touch: tap or hold on a piñata to swing

**Suggested category / tags:** Clicker / Idle; tags: clicker, 3D, casual, upgrade, smash, party, kids

**Orientation:** Landscape (portrait works too)

**Mobile:** Supported (touch)
