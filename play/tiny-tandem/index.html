How to play

Controls

Solo: ←→ or AD move, Space jump, ↓ enter door, Q switch buddy, F follow on/off

P1 AD move, W jump, S enter or drop key

P2 arrow keys, ↓ enter

P3 JL move, I jump, K enter

P4 FH move, T jump, G enter (or numpad 4 6 8 5)

Gamepads work too: pad 1 is P1, pad 2 is P2. R restart, Esc pause, N show hint, M music.

The rules

Carry the key to the door, then everyone steps inside. Nobody gets left behind.

Stand on heads to reach high places. Numbers on lifts, crates and plates show how many buddies they need.

Colored walls let only their matching buddy through. Swap switches make everyone trade places. Portals link in pairs.

Later worlds rope you together, link your jumps, and turn the lights off.
