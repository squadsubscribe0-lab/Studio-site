Tap an arrow and it leaves the board tip first, its body following its own shape. It only goes if the straight lane ahead of the tip is completely empty.

Tap a blocked arrow and it crashes into whatever is in the way, costing a heart.

Clearing an arrow only ever opens space, so you can't lock yourself out — the challenge is reading the board, not move order.
