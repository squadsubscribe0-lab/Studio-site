How to play

  1. Your cards lie face down. Tap any one to flip it.
  2. A number card gives you that many moves in a row. A 3 means you move three times, with any of your pieces.
  3. For each move, tap a highlighted piece, then tap a dot.

Special cards don't give moves. They do something else instead, shown on the Cards tab.

Capture the enemy king to win
  • With a timer on, your clock only runs while it's your turn to decide. If it reaches zero, you lose.
  • There's no check. Keep your king safe, because your rival can get 3 moves too.
  • Revived pieces go back to their starting squares, or the nearest free square on your back rows.
  • After a Reverse, the board turns so your new pieces are always at the bottom.
  • A pawn that reaches the far side becomes a queen.

Chess pieces: Chessnut by Alexis Luengas (Apache 2.0)
