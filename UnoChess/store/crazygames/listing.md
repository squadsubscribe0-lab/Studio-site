# Wild Gambit — CrazyGames portal text

## Title
Wild Gambit

## Short description (one line)
Chess where a card decides your turn: flip it and make 1, 2 or 3 moves in a row.

## Description
Wild Gambit is chess with a deck of cards. On your turn you flip one of your face-down cards, and the card decides what you get. A number card gives you that many moves in a row, with any of your pieces. Draw a 3 and you can push a pawn, jump a knight and swing your queen into an attack before your rival gets to move.

Special cards turn the game upside down. Skip gives you an extra turn. +2 brings back two of your captured pieces. Wild lets you pick 1, 2 or 3 moves. Reverse makes both players swap colours, and the board turns around so you now play the other army.

There's no check. You win by capturing the enemy king, so keep yours safe: your rival can draw a 3 too.

Features:
- Play against a bot (Easy or Normal)
- 2 players on one device
- Play online with a friend using a room code or invite link
- Optional chess clock: 3, 5 or 10 minutes, or no timer
- Three board themes: Wood, Green and Blue
- Works on desktop and mobile, in portrait and landscape

## Controls
Mouse or touch only:
- Click or tap one of your face-down cards to flip it
- Click or tap a highlighted piece, then click or tap a dot to move it
- On a Wild card, choose 1, 2 or 3 moves
- ☰ opens the menu (pause, board theme, quit), ? shows the rules, 🔊 toggles sound

## How to play (if the portal has a separate field)
1. Flip one of your face-down cards.
2. Number card (1, 2 or 3): make that many moves in a row, with any pieces.
3. Skip: you play again. +2: two of your captured pieces come back. Wild: pick 1 to 3 moves. Reverse: you swap colours with your rival.
4. There's no check. Capture the enemy king to win. When the timer is on, running out of time loses.
5. A pawn that reaches the far side becomes a queen.
